About

I like making complicated systems easier to trust.

I have spent most of my career around backend services and distributed systems. The part I enjoy most is figuring out what should happen when things do not go perfectly.

How I think about engineering

A request succeeding once is only the beginning. I think about retries, timeouts, duplicate events, slow dependencies, ordering, caching, and what another engineer will need to see when something breaks.

What good backend code means to me

Clear service boundaries, predictable data flow, safe failure handling, useful logs, and code that another engineer can understand without needing a long explanation.
