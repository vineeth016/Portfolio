01 / About

Thoughtful systems.
Right down to the details.

Retries. Timeouts. Ordering. Caching. Data that arrives twice. Services that slow down. Those details are usually where backend systems become either dependable or painful.

What I enjoy

Taking a messy backend problem, drawing the boundaries, deciding what owns what, and making the failure cases clear before they become production surprises.

Backend
APIs · services · data flow

Designing service boundaries, request flows, validation, persistence, caching, and integrations that stay understandable as the system grows.

Distributed
events · concurrency · reliability

Working with asynchronous systems where retries, idempotency, backpressure, ordering, and failure recovery matter as much as the happy path.

Platform
cloud · delivery · observability

Getting services into production cleanly with containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, monitoring, and enough visibility to know what is actually happening.
